# Raed Robot | روبوت رائد 🤖

**Second Place — Sinna’aThon 2024**  
**Team project | Industrial safety robotics prototype**

## Overview
Raed is a collaborative robotics prototype developed to explore AI-assisted checks of personal protective equipment (PPE) in industrial workplaces. The team assembled a physical robot and worked with a **HuskyLens AI camera** trained to recognize safety equipment. The project concept includes checking items such as helmets, safety glasses, and gloves, and alerting users when equipment is missing.

## My contributions
- Collaborated in assembling the physical robot and integrating its hardware components.
- Participated in training the HuskyLens camera to recognize safety equipment.
- Contributed to the team's development and demonstration of the prototype.

**Team credit:** Arduino code was written by another team member. Hardware assembly and camera training were collaborative. This repository does not claim individual authorship of the full robot system.
